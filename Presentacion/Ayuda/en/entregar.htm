Warehouse Delivery

What it is for

Records the hand-over of the items of a paid invoice (CUN-005). Each invoice is handed over only once.

Who uses it

Warehouse clerk

Where it is

Warehouse → Deliver Items

Step by step

  1. Open Warehouse → Deliver Items.
  2. Type the invoice number the customer shows and press Validate.
  3. The items to hand over are shown with their quantity, unit and warehouse location. Prepare them.
  4. Press Register delivery and hand the items to the customer.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
Receipt No.Number of the invoice to hand over.
ValidateChecks that the invoice exists, is paid and was not handed over.
Items to deliverItems and quantities to hand over, with their location.
Register deliveryRecords the hand-over and marks the invoice as delivered.

Common messages

MessageWhat to do
Receipt … does not exist.Check the invoice number.
Receipt … was already used to pick up the goods.It cannot be handed over again: look up the invoice at the cash desk.
Cannot deliver more than was invoiced of ….Fix the quantity: it cannot exceed the invoiced amount.
