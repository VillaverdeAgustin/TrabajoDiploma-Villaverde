Validate Order and Charge

What it is for

Validates the order against the payment order, identifies the customer by ID number, collects the payment with the chosen method and issues the invoice (CUN-004). If the customer is not registered, they are added from here (CUN-003, Register customer).

Who uses it

Cashier

Where it is

Checkout → Validate and Charge

Step by step

  1. Open Checkout → Validate and Charge and choose the customer payment order.
  2. Type the ID number and press Find customer. If the customer does not exist, press Register, fill in their data and save it.
  3. Check the order detail against the request.
  4. Choose the payment method, fill in the data it asks for and press Charge and issue receipt.
  5. If the payment is approved, stock is deducted and the invoice is issued and shown on screen. Give a copy to the customer.
