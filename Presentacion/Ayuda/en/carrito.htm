Shopping Cart

What it is for

Builds the customer order at the counter (CUN-001) and then issues the payment order so they pay at the cash desk (CUN-002). For items sold by the meter, the balance of the open spool is checked.

Who uses it

Salesperson

Where it is

Sales → Shopping Cart

Step by step

  1. Open Sales → Shopping Cart.
  2. Search for the item by description, brand, code or category and press Search. Items without stock are highlighted.
  3. Select the item, enter the quantity and press Add to cart. Availability is checked and the total is recalculated.
  4. Repeat until the order is complete and press Confirm cart.
  5. Open Sales → Payment Order, choose the confirmed cart, type the customer name and press Issue.
  6. Give the payment order to the customer so they go to the cash desk.
