Goods Receipt

What it is for

Records the goods received against an issued purchase order (CUN-011): it adds stock, creates the received spools and changes the order status.

Who uses it

Warehouse clerk

Where it is

Purchasing → Goods receipt

Step by step

  1. Open Purchasing → Goods receipt. Issued or partially received orders are listed.
  2. Select the order of the delivery note received.
  3. Type the delivery note number and the quantity received for each line.
  4. Press Register receipt.
