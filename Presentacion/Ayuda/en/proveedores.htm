Suppliers

What it is for

Registers and edits the suppliers of the distributor (CUN-007). The CUIT is validated with its check digit and cannot be repeated.

Who uses it

Owner

Where it is

Purchasing → Suppliers

Step by step

  1. Open Purchasing → Suppliers.
  2. Press New and fill in company name, CUIT, VAT status, line of business, phone, e-mail and address.
  3. Press Save. To edit a supplier, select it in the grid, change the data and save.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
Company nameLegal name of the supplier. Required.
Tax ID11 digits, with or without dashes. The check digit is validated.
VAT statusA supplier cannot be a final consumer.
ActiveUnchecked, the supplier is no longer offered in purchasing (logical delete).

Common messages

MessageWhat to do
Invalid tax IDCheck the CUIT: the 11 digits and the check digit.
A supplier with tax ID … is already registered.The supplier is already registered: find it in the grid.
The email address is not valid.Type an e-mail in the name@domain format.
