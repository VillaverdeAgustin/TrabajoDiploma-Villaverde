Purchases report by supplier

What it is for

Summarizes purchases by supplier over a period: orders, amounts in pesos and dollars, percentage received and pending. The result can be exported to PDF.

Who uses it

Owner

Where it is

Reports → Purchases report

Step by step

  1. Open Reports → Purchases report.
  2. Choose the period and, optionally, the supplier and the status. Press Generate.
  3. Select a supplier to see its orders below.
  4. To save the report, press Export PDF, choose the folder (ReporteCompras_yyyymmdd_hhmm.pdf is suggested) and save. You can open the PDF when it finishes.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
SupplierLimits the report to one supplier.
StatusLimits the report to one order status.
% receivedQuantity received over quantity ordered.
Pending receiptIssued orders with no receipt yet.
Export PDFSaves the generated report as a PDF. It is enabled only when there are results.
