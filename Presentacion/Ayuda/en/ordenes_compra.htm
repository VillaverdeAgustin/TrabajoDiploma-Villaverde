Purchase Orders

What it is for

Builds a supplier purchase order with the items to restock and issues it (CUN-010). The order is left as Issued, ready to be received.

Who uses it

Owner

Where it is

Purchasing → Purchase orders

Step by step

  1. Open Purchasing → Purchase orders. Items at or below the reorder point are shown.
  2. Choose the supplier. Select each item, enter the quantity and press Add to order; to see other prices use Compare prices.
  3. The subtotal of each line is calculated with the supplier current price, and the order total.
  4. Press Generate order. A number is assigned and the order is issued.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
Items to restockItems that reached the reorder point.
SupplierSupplier of the order.
Manual pricePrice from the current list; if there is none, it can be entered by hand.
Draft orderLines of the order not yet issued.
Generate orderIssues the purchase order.

Common messages

MessageWhat to do
The supplier has no current price for the item.Import the supplier list or enter the price by hand.
There is no draft order for the supplier.Add at least one item before generating the order.
The order changed while it was being edited: reload it.Close and reopen the screen to reload the order.
