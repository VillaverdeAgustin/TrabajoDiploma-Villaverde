Import Price List

What it is for

Imports a supplier price list from an .xlsx or .csv file, with its effective date and currency (CUN-008). It does not change the sale price of the items.

Who uses it

Owner

Where it is

Purchasing → Import price list

Step by step

  1. Open Purchasing → Import price list.
  2. Choose the supplier, the effective date and the currency.
  3. Press Browse..., choose the file and press Preview. The lines are shown: existing, new and rejected with their reason.
  4. Press Import. The prices are saved and the summary is shown.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
SupplierSupplier that owns the list. If it does not exist, use New supplier.
Effective dateDate from which the prices apply.
CurrencyARS or USD.
File.xlsx or .csv file with code, description, price, unit and delivery time.
PreviewReads the file and shows what will be imported, without saving anything.
ImportSaves the previewed list.
