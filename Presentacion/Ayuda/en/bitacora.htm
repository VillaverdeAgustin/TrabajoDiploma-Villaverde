Event Log

What it is for

Looks up the events recorded by the system: logins, creations, changes, payments, purchases, backups and other actions, with their user and date.

Who uses it

Administrator

Where it is

Admin → Event Log

Step by step

  1. Open Admin → Event Log.
  2. Choose the user, the event and the date range, and press Search.
  3. To start another search, press Clear.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
UserUser who generated the event.
EventType of action recorded.
Date rangeDate range to look up.
