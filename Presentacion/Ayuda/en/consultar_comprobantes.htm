Search invoices

What it is for

Looks up invoices already issued and shows them in full, with detail, totals and payments (CUN-006).

Who uses it

Cashier

Where it is

Checkout → Search invoices

Step by step

  1. Open Checkout → Search invoices.
  2. Type the invoice number or the customer data (name, ID number or CUIT); optionally set a date range. Press Search.
  3. Select an invoice and press View invoice or double-click it.
  4. The invoice is shown. For final consumers it shows the VAT included (Tax Transparency Regime). Press Close.
