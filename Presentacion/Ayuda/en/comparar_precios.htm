Compare Prices

What it is for

Compares the current prices of an item among suppliers and adds it to the draft purchase order of the one you choose (CUN-009).

Who uses it

Owner

Where it is

Purchasing → Compare prices

Step by step

  1. Open Purchasing → Compare prices.
  2. Type the description, brand or code, choose the currency and press Search.
  3. Choose the item: suppliers are listed with price, effective date, delivery time and sale unit, from cheapest to most expensive.
  4. Select the supplier, enter the quantity and press Add to order.

Fields and buttons

On screenMeaning
Description or codeText to search in description, brand or code.
CurrencyOnly prices from lists in that currency are compared.
Current prices by supplierPrices by supplier. Expired lists are marked.
Add to orderAdds the item to the draft purchase order of the chosen supplier.

Common messages

MessageWhat to do
No suppliers were found for the itemImport a price list that includes the item.
The item is already in the draft purchase order for that supplier.The item is already in that draft: change the quantity in Purchase orders.
The quantity must be greater than zero.Type a quantity greater than zero.
