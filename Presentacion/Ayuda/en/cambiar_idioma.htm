Change language

What it is for

Changes the language of every open screen instantly: Spanish or English. The help also opens in the chosen language.

Who uses it

All users

Where it is

User → Change Language

Step by step

  1. Open User → Change Language and choose the language.
  2. You can also choose it on the login screen, or in Admin → Languages.
  3. The texts of the open screens change without closing them.
